# Grade 3 Math: 36-Unit Scope and Sequence

This document defines the 36-unit, 180-day scope and sequence for Grade 3 LumaMath. Each unit contains five lessons: four teaching/practice lessons followed by an evaluation and spiral review. Unit numbers are intended for parent visibility; children see concept titles such as “Building Big Numbers” rather than “Unit 2.”

## Part 1: Place Value and Addition

### Unit 1: Place Value Foundations

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.2

- What a digit’s place tells us
- Build numbers with base-ten models
- Write numbers in standard and expanded form
- Read and write numbers in words
- **Unit Evaluation:** Place Value Foundations

### Unit 2: Numbers Through 100,000

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.2

- Thousands and ten-thousands
- Read and identify numbers through 100,000
- Expanded form through 100,000
- Number names and place-value puzzles
- **Unit Evaluation:** Numbers Through 100,000

### Unit 3: Rounding and Estimation

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.1, beginning 3.RA.D.10

- Round to the nearest ten using a number line
- Round to the nearest hundred using a number line
- Use place value to round numbers
- Estimate and identify reasonable answers
- **Unit Evaluation:** Rounding and Estimation

### Unit 4: Addition Strategies

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.3

- Add three-digit numbers on a number line
- Add using expanded form
- Add using compensation
- Add without regrouping and solve word problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Addition Strategies

### Unit 5: Addition With Regrouping

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.3

- Regroup ones when adding
- Regroup tens when adding
- Add three numbers
- Missing digits, balanced equations, and addition properties
- **Unit Evaluation:** Addition Within 1,000

## Part 2: Subtraction and Mixed Operations

### Unit 6: Subtraction Strategies

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.3

- Subtract three-digit numbers on a number line
- Subtract using expanded form
- Subtract using compensation
- Subtract without regrouping and solve word problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Subtraction Strategies

### Unit 7: Subtraction With Regrouping

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.3

- Regroup ones when subtracting
- Regroup tens when subtracting
- Subtract across zeros
- Missing digits and balanced subtraction equations
- **Unit Evaluation:** Subtraction Within 1,000

### Unit 8: Addition and Subtraction Problem Solving

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.3, 3.RA.D.9, 3.RA.D.10

- Decide whether to add or subtract
- Estimate before solving
- One-step addition and subtraction problems
- Two-step problems with unknown numbers
- **Unit Evaluation:** Addition and Subtraction Applications

## Part 3: Multiplication Foundations

### Unit 9: Equal Groups

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.1, 3.RA.A.3

- Count equal groups
- Connect repeated addition to multiplication
- Identify factors and products
- Draw and describe multiplication situations
- **Unit Evaluation:** Equal Groups

### Unit 10: Arrays and Number Lines

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.1, 3.RA.A.3

- Build arrays to represent multiplication
- Write two multiplication equations for an array
- Represent multiplication on a number line
- Connect models, equations, and stories
- **Unit Evaluation:** Multiplication Models

### Unit 11: Multiplying by 0, 1, 2, 5, and 10

**Standards:** 3.RA.B.6, 3.RA.C.7

- Zero and identity rules
- Multiply by 2 using doubles
- Multiply by 5 using patterns
- Multiply by 10 and practice mixed facts
- **Unit Evaluation:** Facts for 0, 1, 2, 5, and 10

### Unit 12: Multiplying by 3 and 4

**Standards:** 3.RA.B.6, 3.RA.C.7

- Multiply by 3
- Multiply by 4 using doubles
- Use the commutative property
- Use grouping and the associative property
- **Unit Evaluation:** Facts for 3 and 4

## Part 4: Division Foundations

### Unit 13: Understanding Division

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.2, 3.RA.A.3

- Division as sharing equally
- Division as counting equal groups
- Write division equations for groups
- Division stories involving 1 and 0
- **Unit Evaluation:** Meaning of Division

### Unit 14: Connecting Multiplication and Division

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.2, 3.RA.B.6

- Model division with arrays
- Model division on number lines
- Build multiplication and division fact families
- Use multiplication to solve division problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Multiplication and Division Connections

### Unit 15: Facts for 6 and 7

**Standards:** 3.RA.B.6, 3.RA.C.7

- Build ×6 facts from known facts
- Divide by 6
- Build ×7 facts using strategies
- Divide by 7 and practice related facts
- **Unit Evaluation:** Facts for 6 and 7

### Unit 16: Facts for 8 and 9

**Standards:** 3.RA.B.6, 3.RA.C.7

- Multiply by 8 using doubles
- Divide by 8
- Multiply by 9 using patterns
- Divide by 9 and practice related facts
- **Unit Evaluation:** Facts for 8 and 9

### Unit 17: Multiplication and Division Fluency

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.5, 3.RA.C.7, 3.RA.C.8

- Practice mixed multiplication facts
- Find missing factors
- Find missing numbers in division equations
- Choose efficient fact strategies
- **Unit Evaluation:** Facts Within 100

### Unit 18: Multiplication and Division Problems

**Standards:** 3.RA.A.4, 3.RA.D.9, 3.RA.E.11

- Solve equal-group and array problems
- Use strip models
- Write equations with unknown numbers
- Solve two-step problems and identify patterns
- **Unit Evaluation:** Multiplication and Division Applications

### Unit 19: Multiplying by Multiples of Ten

**Standards:** 3.NBT.A.4, 3.RA.E.11

- Connect basic facts to multiples of ten
- Multiply one-digit numbers by multiples of ten
- Solve real-world problems with multiples of ten
- Explain place-value and multiplication patterns
- **Unit Evaluation:** Multiples of Ten

## Part 5: Area and Perimeter

### Unit 20: Understanding Area

**Standards:** 3.GM.C.9, 3.GM.C.10

- Area as the space inside a shape
- Cover figures with unit squares
- Count and label square units
- Find area when some squares are hidden
- **Unit Evaluation:** Area With Unit Squares

### Unit 21: Area and Multiplication

**Standards:** 3.GM.C.11, 3.GM.C.12

- Tile rectangles with unit squares
- Connect rows and columns to multiplication
- Find the area of rectangles and squares
- Solve area word problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Multiplying to Find Area

### Unit 22: Area Arrangements and Decomposition

**Standards:** 3.GM.C.13, 3.GM.C.14

- Create rectangles with a given area
- Find different arrangements for the same area
- Find a missing side length
- Decompose rectangles using the distributive property
- **Unit Evaluation:** Area Strategies

### Unit 23: Understanding Perimeter

**Standards:** 3.GM.D.15

- Perimeter as distance around a shape
- Find perimeter on grids
- Find the perimeter of rectangles and quadrilaterals
- Find missing side lengths and solve perimeter problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Perimeter

### Unit 24: Area and Perimeter Relationships

**Standards:** 3.GM.D.16

- Same perimeter, different areas
- Same area, different perimeters
- Find area, perimeter, or a missing side
- Use area and perimeter to solve real-world problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Area and Perimeter

## Part 6: Graphs and Data

### Unit 25: Picture Graphs and Bar Graphs

**Standards:** 3.DS.A.1, 3.DS.A.2

- Read scaled picture graphs
- Create scaled picture graphs
- Read scaled bar graphs
- Create graphs and solve one- and two-step problems
- **Unit Evaluation:** Scaled Graphs

## Part 7: Fraction Foundations

### Unit 26: Equal Parts and Unit Fractions

**Standards:** 3.GM.A.3, 3.NF.A.1

- Identify equal and unequal parts
- Make halves, thirds, and fourths
- Make sixths and eighths
- Name unit fractions of partitioned shapes
- **Unit Evaluation:** Equal Parts and Unit Fractions

### Unit 27: Numerators and Denominators

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.1, 3.NF.A.2

- Understand what the numerator represents
- Understand what the denominator represents
- Represent fractions with fraction bars
- Represent fractions with area models and stories
- **Unit Evaluation:** Naming Fractions

### Unit 28: Fractions on Number Lines

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.3

- Understand the interval from 0 to 1
- Partition number lines into equal lengths
- Locate unit fractions on number lines
- Locate and graph non-unit fractions
- **Unit Evaluation:** Fractions on Number Lines

### Unit 29: Equivalent Fractions With Models

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.5

- Understand equivalence as the same amount
- Find equivalent fractions using fraction strips
- Find equivalent fractions using area models
- Generate and explain equivalent fractions
- **Unit Evaluation:** Equivalent Fraction Models

### Unit 30: Equivalent Fractions on Number Lines

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.4, 3.NF.A.5

- Identify fractions at the same number-line location
- Find equivalents using number lines
- Graph equivalent fractions
- Connect models, number lines, and equations
- **Unit Evaluation:** Equivalent Fractions

### Unit 31: Comparing Fractions With Like Denominators

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.6, supporting 3.NF.A.7

- Compare fractions using visual models
- Compare fractions using number lines
- Use <, >, and =
- Solve comparison problems and justify answers
- **Unit Evaluation:** Like Denominators

### Unit 32: Comparing Fractions With Like Numerators

**Standards:** 3.NF.A.6, 3.NF.A.7

- Compare fractions with the same numerator using models
- Compare fractions with the same numerator on number lines
- Understand why fractions must refer to the same whole
- Compare fractions and explain the reasoning
- **Unit Evaluation:** Fraction Comparison

## Part 8: Geometry and Measurement

### Unit 33: Shapes and Quadrilaterals

**Standards:** 3.GM.A.1, 3.GM.A.2

- Describe shapes using sides and vertices
- Identify parallel sides and quadrilaterals
- Classify squares, rectangles, and rhombuses
- Classify and draw parallelograms and trapezoids
- **Unit Evaluation:** Quadrilaterals

### Unit 34: Measurement and Line Plots

**Standards:** 3.GM.B.7, 3.DS.A.3, 3.DS.A.4

- Choose and estimate customary length units
- Measure to the nearest quarter inch
- Choose units for weight, mass, and liquid volume
- Create and interpret line plots, including fractional data
- **Unit Evaluation:** Measurement Data

### Unit 35: Time and Elapsed Time

**Standards:** 3.GM.B.4, 3.GM.B.5, 3.GM.B.6

- Read analog clocks to the nearest minute
- Match analog, digital, and written times
- Estimate time intervals in minutes
- Find elapsed time and ending time
- **Unit Evaluation:** Time

## Part 9: Integrated Application

### Unit 36: Measurement and Four-Operation Problem Solving

**Standards:** 3.GM.B.8, 3.RA.D.9, 3.RA.D.10, review of 3.RA.E.11

- Solve length, weight, and liquid-volume problems
- Solve mixed measurement problems in the same units
- Write and solve two-step equations with unknowns
- Estimate, solve, and evaluate reasonableness
- **Cumulative Grade 3 Evaluation**

## Standards That Continue Across Multiple Units

Some standards should not be treated as concepts that disappear after one unit.

### Multiplication Fluency

**3.RA.C.8** begins during Unit 11 and continues through the remainder of the year through short reviews, games, and spaced practice.

### Two-Step Problem Solving

**3.RA.D.9** begins with addition and subtraction in Unit 8, expands to multiplication and division in Unit 18, and culminates in Unit 36.

### Estimation and Reasonableness

**3.RA.D.10** begins with rounding in Unit 3 and should appear regularly in arithmetic and word-problem units.

### Arithmetic Patterns

**3.RA.E.11** should recur in:

- Place-value patterns
- Multiplication tables
- Multiples of ten
- Input/output tables
- Real-world multiplication and division patterns

### Fraction Comparison and the Same-Whole Rule

**3.NF.A.7** should be introduced before formal comparison and reinforced every time students compare fractions.

## Recommended Lesson Rhythm

Each unit can use the same predictable LumaMath rhythm:

1. **Discover:** Introduce the idea through models or a meaningful situation
2. **Build:** Manipulate objects, diagrams, arrays, number lines, or shapes
3. **Connect:** Move from the visual model to equations and mathematical language
4. **Apply:** Practice, explain, and solve a real-world problem
5. **Show What You Know:** Unit evaluation plus cumulative spiral review

This gives the curriculum a steady heartbeat without making the child feel trapped inside an endless worksheet conveyor belt.
